Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Strays is a Mac app that finds, plays and helps you clear photos, videos and music on your own computer.

What Strays reads

Only the folders you choose in the folder picker, including folders you pin. Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Music and TV are read only after you turn them on in Folder Access. It reads file names, sizes, dates and the files themselves so it can show thumbnails and play them.

What Strays stores

A list of the files it found and small preview images, saved on your Mac inside the app's own storage so the app opens quickly next time. You can clear the list in Strays → Settings → Advanced.

What Strays sends

Nothing. Strays makes no network connections, has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. None of your files or information leave your Mac.

Changes to your files

Strays moves, trashes or deletes files only when you ask it to. Permanent deletion always asks for confirmation first.
