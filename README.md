# Strays

Every lost photo, video and song on your Mac, found.

Strays looks through the folders you choose and brings every photo, video and song into one calm library. See how much space each type takes, play anything with a click, and clear out what you no longer need.

- **Find:** one scan shows every photo, video and song, plus your biggest files, screenshots, media in Downloads, things untouched for two years, and possible duplicates.
- **Play:** photos full size, videos and music in a built-in player.
- **Clear:** select several files, then show them in Finder, move them to the Trash, or delete them permanently (always asks first).
- **Private:** reads only the folders you allow. Nothing is uploaded.

Developer folders (anything with a `.git`, `package.json`, `Package.swift` and similar) are skipped by default, so app assets never show up or get deleted by mistake.

## Install

```sh
brew trust hyppe-cc/tap      # newer Homebrew asks you to trust third-party taps once
brew install --cask hyppe-cc/tap/strays
```

Or download `Strays-1.0.zip` from [Releases](https://github.com/hyppe-cc/strays/releases). Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Signed and notarized by Apple.
