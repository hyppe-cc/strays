Strays

Every lost photo, video and song on your Mac, found.

Strays looks through the folders you choose and brings your media into one calm library. See how much space each type takes, play anything with a click, and clear what you no longer need.

Install

Free with Homebrew:

brew trust hyppe-cc/tap
brew install --cask hyppe-cc/tap/strays

Or download it from GitHub Releases. Coming to the Mac App Store for $2.99. Requires macOS 14 or later.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: fervillanuev4s@gmail.com

Files you expected are missing? Strays only reads folders you allow, skips code projects, and hides tiny files by default. Change this in Strays → Settings.

Deleted something by mistake? Move to Trash can be undone from the Trash. Delete Permanently can't be undone, which is why it always asks first.
